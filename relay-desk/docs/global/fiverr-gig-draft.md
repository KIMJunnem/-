## 2. 설명 (Description) — 1,109자

**English**

```
Need Korean subtitles that stay in sync all the way through?

A common problem with auto-generated subtitles: they look fine at the start, then slowly drift and end up seconds off by the middle of the video. I check timing across the whole video, from start to finish (not a quick spot check), and fix drift where it starts.

What I offer
- Foreign-language video -> Korean subtitles (SRT): English or French audio. Other languages: please ask before ordering.
- Korean video -> Korean subtitles (SRT)
- Optional: subtitles burned into your video (MP4)

How I work
I use AI tools for the first transcript and draft translation. Then I review the result as a native Korean speaker, rewrite lines that sound unnatural to Korean viewers, and check timing against the audio. Before delivery, the timing is checked against the source from start to finish.

Included
- SRT file ready for YouTube, Premiere, DaVinci, etc.
- 2 revisions

Before you order, please message me with your video length and language. If your language or audio quality is something I can't handle well, I'll tell you honestly before you pay.
```

## 5. FAQ 5개

**Q1. Do you use AI? / AI를 쓰나요?**
- EN: Yes. I use AI tools for the first transcript and the draft translation. Every file is then reviewed by me, a native Korean speaker, and checked for timing before delivery. I don't claim it's 100% handwritten.
- KO: 네. 첫 전사와 번역 초안에는 AI 도구를 씁니다. 그 뒤 한국어 원어민인 제가 모든 파일을 검토하고 납품 전에 싱크를 확인합니다. 100% 손으로 썼다고 말씀드리지 않습니다.

**Q2. What does "full sync check" mean? / "전 구간 싱크 검수"가 뭔가요?**
- EN: Auto subtitles often drift a few seconds by the middle of a video. I check the timing from start to finish, not just a few samples, and fix the point where the drift begins.
- KO: 자동 자막은 영상 중간쯤 몇 초씩 밀리는 경우가 많습니다. 몇 군데만 보는 게 아니라 처음부터 끝까지 타이밍을 확인하고, 밀리기 시작한 지점을 고칩니다.

**Q3. Which languages can you translate from? / 어떤 언어를 번역하나요?**
- EN: English and French. For any other language, please message me before ordering. I'll check whether I can deliver good quality for that language, and if I can't, I'll say so before you pay.
- KO: 영어와 프랑스어입니다. 다른 언어는 주문 전에 메시지로 알려 주세요. 그 언어로 좋은 품질을 낼 수 있는지 확인해서, 어렵다면 결제 전에 말씀드립니다.
- (9/23 준희 확정: 영어·프랑스어만 명시, 나머지는 주문 전 문의)

**Q4. What if I'm not happy with some lines? / 일부 줄이 마음에 안 들면요?**
- EN: Every package includes 2 revisions. Tell me the timecode or the line, and I'll fix it.
- KO: 모든 패키지에 수정 2회가 포함됩니다. 시간(타임코드)이나 해당 줄을 알려 주시면 고쳐 드립니다.

**Q5. My video is longer than 30 minutes. / 영상이 30분보다 길어요.**
- EN: Send me a message with the length, and I'll send you a custom offer.
- KO: 길이를 메시지로 알려 주시면 맞춤 견적(Custom offer)을 보내 드립니다.
